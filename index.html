What is Artificial Intelligence?

Artificial intelligence (AI) is the ability for computers to run algorithms and perform tasks mimicing human intelligence. There are many different types of AI models, a variety of ways to integrate it into technology, and tons of use cases. The ability to mimic human intelligence in computers is very useful, because where basic traditional algorithms fail, AI models can bring greater precision and accuracy. For example, classification is one of the biggest applications of artificial intelligence. Normal algorithms often have to be hard-coded with conditions that can help with classification. If a normal algorithm needed to classify dogs, cats, birds, and fish, it could possibly be coded to eliminate birds and fish as options if there was fur, or it takes wings as automatically being a bird. However, exceptions (like fish fins looking like wings, or feathers looking like fur) and the vast amount of different types of these animals, the angles a picture could be taken at, and the differences of lighting in different pictures could all affect the accuracy. In addition, physically hard-coding this would be difficult, because it is hard to tell a computer what a wing is, or what fur looks like. Artificial intelligence, on the other hand, doesn't rely on pre-coded conditions. Instead, it trains son data to identify patterns, whether it is a one dimensional relationship or a boundary in higher dimensions.
