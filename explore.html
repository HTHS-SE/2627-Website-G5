Why Artificial Intelligence?

You may have heard people saying that artificial intelligence is the future of technology. The biggest questions that people have are: Why should we continue to develop AI, and will it have a positive effect? Many have raised concerns about how AI's affect on humanity. There have been cases of data centers depleting local water sources, which is the least of our worries. AI researchers and experts in the field are worried that humanity will become too dependent on AI, and some even believe that there is actually a fairly substantial chance that AI will bring about the end of humanity.


If all these problems could stem from the development and use of artificial intelligence, why should we continue to perpetuate and improve these models? Well, there is a reason that new technologies are created and improved: to make life easier for people. Before Gutenberg's printing press revolutionized the publishing industry in 1440, people had to transcribe pieces of literature by hand. Over 440 years later, the linotype machine was developed to further automate the printing process. Today's modern inkjet printerns are the most common commercially available printers, with many being able to wirelessly print pages with the click of a button. But what does this have to do with AI?


As we have seen over the course of the past few years, artificial intelligence models can be used to speed up and/or automate various processes, like programming, document analysis, and mathematical problem solving. This productivity efficiency has never been matched before by any technological advancements. And the best part about this is that it is incredibly easy to learn how to make and operate artificial inteligence models. This website serves as an educational tool to teach people about AI and how it can be applied to our everyday lives.
